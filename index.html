How a request moves

  1. You choose a service

    Pick the service that matches what you need.

  2. You describe the issue

    Registration number, subject and description go on one form.

  3. The right office receives it

    Each category maps to the office that handles it.

  4. You get a reference

    Every request carries a reference for follow-up.
